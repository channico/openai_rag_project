Employees receive twenty days of paid vacation each calendar year. Vacation requests should normally be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Managers approve requests based on team coverage and current project commitments.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote working days should be agreed upon with the employee's manager. Employees must remain available during the team's normal working hours and attend required meetings.

The company provides each employee with a laptop and necessary accessories. Equipment remains company property and must be returned when employment ends. Lost or damaged equipment should be reported to the IT department immediately.

Business travel must be approved before reservations are made. Employees should use the company's approved booking system. Reasonable transportation, lodging, and meal expenses will be reimbursed when accompanied by receipts.

Employees experiencing technical problems should contact the IT help desk. Critical security incidents, including lost devices or suspected account compromise, must be reported immediately. Routine support requests are normally answered within one business day.
